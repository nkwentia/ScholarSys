Series (e.g. Arts / Science)

Define the series your school uses to group streams and subjects — e.g. Arts and Science. Once defined, tag each stream with a Series below (in Custom Classes & Streams) and tag each subject with the Series it belongs to. A class tagged with a Series will only ever be scheduled subjects belonging to that Series — so Form 4A (Arts) and Form 4B (Science) can never be assigned each other's subjects. Applies to any class that has streams. Leave a stream or subject untagged to keep it unrestricted.

Assign Subjects to a Series

Tick every series a subject belongs to. A subject with no ticks is unrestricted and can run in any class, tagged or not (useful for compulsory subjects like Mathematics/English). A subject tagged to exactly ONE series can never be auto-paired by the timetable generator — even with a matching Can-Pair rule, it will only be placed in a shared slot if you add it there yourself in the timetable. Tag a subject to BOTH series (or leave it untagged) if you want the generator to auto-pair it.

Required Periods Per Subject Per Class Per Week
How this works: The timetable generator places exactly the number of periods set here for each subject per class. The most accurate source is your teachers' assignment data — use Sync from Teacher Assignments to auto-fill these targets from what your teachers are actually assigned to teach. GCE standard hours are available as a reference baseline if needed.
Pairing is Can-Pair-rule driven only. Two subjects share a period only if you've added a rule for that exact combination under "Can Run in Same Period" above. This applies to every class, including Sixth Form and Form 4/5 option groups — there is no automatic bypass. If your students' real subject combinations aren't fully covered by rules yet, some periods may be left unplaced; add the missing Can-Pair rules to fix that.
